Limitations: this league docks -4 per interception thrown (double the usual), but our data source doesn't include INT counts, so turnover-prone QBs are likely overvalued here — sanity-check QBs manually. The scoring also can't account for 2pt conversions or single-game yardage/TD-length bonuses (needs game logs we don't have), and it's based on last season's production, not a 2026 forecast.
