Rankings blend two data sets. The "+X.X pts" value on each player is Value Over Replacement computed from their actual 2025 stats run through THIS league's exact scoring (0 PPR, 6pt passing TDs, our real roster settings) — this is what actually decides the sort order and the Recommended pick. Live ADP (refreshed via , Fantasy Football Calculator) is layered on top only to flag "won't last" — players our formula likes who the broader market will likely draft before your next turn. Depth chart position and rookie status come from Sleeper. Limitations: the custom scoring can't account for interceptions thrown, 2pt conversions, or single-game yardage/TD-length bonuses (needs game logs we don't have access to), and it's based on last season's production, not a 2026 forecast — so trust it as a strong guide, not gospel, especially for rookies and players in new situations.
